## 🚀 Key Insights from Expert Consultation

### The #1 Gap ALL 5 Experts Identified

**Your playbook was 100% demand-side.** Pronto is a two-sided marketplace, and not one of the original 12 experiments addressed the supply side (house help professionals). Ben Lauzier: *"Supply is the hardest side 80-90% of the time. You're missing out on half of your business."*

### Top 10 Additions (Expert-Recommended)

| # | Addition | Recommended By | Priority |
|---|----------|---------------|----------|
| 1 | **Supply-side growth section** (4 experiments) | ALL 5 | Critical |
| 2 | **Growth model spreadsheet** | Dan, Casey | Critical |
| 3 | **Liquidity metric** | Ben, Dan | Critical |
| 4 | **Founder-led social strategy** | Elena, Albert | High |
| 5 | **Resurrection/reactivation** | Albert, Elena | High |
| 6 | **2-3 more prototypes** | Casey, Elena | High |
| 7 | **Explore/Exploit framework** | Albert | Medium |
| 8 | **"Ship this week" section** | Casey | Medium |
| 9 | **Competitive moat section** | Casey | Medium |
| 10 | **"Don't do this" section** | Dan | Medium |

### Key Divergences

- **First booking**: Elena says make it **FREE** (not 30% off) — treat as marketing spend
- **SEO**: Elena says **deprioritize** — organic is now social, not search
- **Subscriptions**: Casey warns Pronto Plus needs hard unit economics or it cannibalizes
- **Approach**: Elena says flip to 95% innovation; Dan says be cautious — marketplaces are gardens
